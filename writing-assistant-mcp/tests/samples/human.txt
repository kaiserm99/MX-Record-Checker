I've worked from home since March 2020 and honestly I still can't decide if I like it. Some weeks are great. I get up at 7, walk the dog, and I'm at my desk by 8 with a coffee and no commute, which saves me about 90 minutes a day.

Other weeks it's awful. My team is spread across Berlin, Lisbon and Austin, so there's always someone waiting on an answer from someone who's asleep. We tried a "no meetings on Friday" rule last year. It lasted three weeks.

The thing nobody warned me about was the kitchen. When your office is ten steps from the fridge you eat constantly, and I put on 6 kilos in the first year. My fix was dumb but it worked: I moved my desk upstairs.

Would I go back to the office full time? No. Two days a week, maybe, if the coffee's decent.

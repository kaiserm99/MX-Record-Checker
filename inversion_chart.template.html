Hourly values as a table

Model data from the Open-Meteo forecast API, downscaled to the site elevations. These are model estimates, not station observations.

Inversion means the upper site is warmer than the lower one (lapse rate above 0 °C per 100 m). The cloud deck is the layer of the model column with relative humidity of 85 % or more. Wind is the 10 m mean (thick) and gust (thin) speed. Fog score is a 0 to 10 heuristic from dew-point spread, humidity, visibility, low cloud and wind.
